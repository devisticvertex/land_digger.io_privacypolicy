1 1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Land Digger.io! This Privacy Policy describes how Devistic Vertex ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal and technical information when you download, install, play, or interact with our mobile game application Land Digger.io (Android Application ID: com.devisticvertex.landdigger.io).

Land Digger.io is an arcade territory-conquest casual game where players dig excavation trails across an arena grid, capture land, outmaneuver rivals, collect custom skins, and participate in World Tour challenges.

We are committed to operating in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement & Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

ℹ️
Summary: By downloading, installing, or playing Land Digger.io, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, you may discontinue use of the game or delete your account at any time.

2 2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver smooth gameplay, preserve your skins and high scores, and provide reliable account services.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Player Display Name: You can choose and edit a custom nickname (3–16 characters) shown above your digger in matches and on the arena leaderboard. This does not have to be your real legal name.
  • Google Account Profile (Optional): If you choose to link your account via Google Sign-In, we receive authentication tokens, your public profile name, your email address, and a unique user identifier provided through Google OAuth and Supabase Auth.
  • Support Inquiries: If you contact our support team via email, we receive your email address, device model, and any correspondence you provide to help resolve your issue.

B. Information Collected Automatically During Gameplay

  • Game Progression & Statistics: Match outcomes, maximum percentage of territory captured, bot elimination count ("kills"), survival duration, match rank, classic/timed game modes, difficulty preferences, and World Tour stage completions.
  • Virtual Economy Balance: In-game coin balance, gem balance, unlocked skins, currently equipped digger skin, and daily login reward streaks.
  • Device & Technical Data: Operating system version (Android), device manufacturer and model, screen resolution, language settings, and general IP-derived country code (2-letter ISO code, e.g., US, PK, DE) used for regional leaderboard groupings.
  • Advertising Identifier: Google Advertising ID (AAID) subject to your device settings and consent, used for ad fraud prevention and delivery of rewarded videos.
🔒
Safe Public Profiles: Our cloud database uses a protected view (public_profiles) for leaderboards that exposes only your nickname, equipped skin, and match records. Your private coin wallet, gem balances, and email are never publicly exposed to other players.

3 3. App Permissions & Why We Need Them

Land Digger.io requests only the standard Android permissions strictly required to execute core gameplay and cloud features. We do not ask for camera, contacts, storage, or microphone access:

Permission Name Category Purpose & Justification
android.permission.INTERNET Network Required to connect with Supabase cloud servers, synchronize player scores, authenticate with Google Sign-In, and verify in-app purchases.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Network Detects whether the device is connected to Wi-Fi, cellular, or offline so the game can switch smoothly to offline/guest mode.
android.permission.VIBRATE Hardware Triggers tactile haptic feedback during digs, collisions, loop captures, and UI button presses (can be toggled off in Settings).
com.android.vending.BILLING In-App Billing Enables the Google Play In-App Purchase dialog for buying optional coin packages, gem packs, and cosmetic skins.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK Hardware Prevents your phone or tablet screen from dimming or going to sleep during active excavation gameplay matches.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Advertising Required on Android 13+ (API 33+) by Google Mobile Ads SDK for attribution, frequency capping, and ad fraud detection.
android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Audio Adjusts master sound effects (SFX) and background music (BGM) levels based on your in-game audio sliders.

4 4. How We Use Collected Data

We process information exclusively for legitimate game operations and enhancement:

  • Delivering Core Gameplay: Generating territory maps, computing bounded polygon flood-fills, synchronizing bot behaviors, and rendering real-time radar minimaps.
  • Cross-Device Cloud Sync: Preserving your unlocked skins, rank roads, and currency balance across device changes or game reinstallations.
  • Fair Play & Leaderboards: Calculating global rankings, detecting fraudulent score submissions, and ensuring a balanced competitive environment.
  • In-App Economy Management: Fulfilling Google Play digital goods purchases and awarding daily reward calendar streaks.
  • Bug Fixing & Stability: Diagnosing game engine crashes, optimizing frame rates (targeting 60–120 FPS), and improving offline simulation caches.
  • Communication: Responding directly to your technical support inquiries, bug reports, and account recovery requests.

6 6. In-App Purchases & Virtual Currency

Land Digger.io offers optional in-game purchases through the Google Play Store (e.g., Coin Bundles, Gem Pouches, All-Skins Pack).

  • No Real Money Value: Coins, gems, and skins are strictly virtual entertainment items within Land Digger.io. They carry no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged, transferred, or redeemed for cash.
  • Google Payment Processing: All financial transactions are securely handled by Google Play. We do not collect, view, or retain credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing addresses.
  • Audit Logging: Our database maintains a secure transaction audit log (purchases_log) recording the product ID and Google Play order ID to ensure purchased items can be restored reliably upon reinstallation.

7 7. Advertising & AdMob Policy

Land Digger.io is designed with player satisfaction as top priority. While the game provides an enjoyable experience without mandatory advertisements, optional ad placements (such as Rewarded Video Ads for bonus coins or a 7-Day Ad-Free Pass) may be powered by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including Google Advertising ID), coarse location based on IP, and ad interaction data to provide appropriate ad content and prevent click fraud.

8 8. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Land Digger.io is intended as a family-friendly casual game suitable for general audiences. Protecting children’s privacy online is of paramount importance to us.

  • We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area / UK).
  • Children can play the entire game as guests without creating an account or providing an email address.
  • All advertising configurations within the app are set to comply with Google Play’s Families Policy and COPPA requirements, restricting behavioral profiling for younger users.
  • If a parent or legal guardian discovers that their child has provided personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately at support@devisticvertex.com. We will promptly delete that information from our servers.

9 9. Data Retention & Security Measures

We employ robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your game data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Land Digger.io mobile client and our backend servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Database Row-Level Security (RLS): Our PostgreSQL cloud database enforces strict cryptographic row-level access control. Only authenticated players can view or update their private inventory.
  • Data Retention Period: Guest gameplay records are retained locally on your device. Cloud account profiles are retained for as long as your account remains active or until you request account deletion.

10 10. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location (such as the European Union, United Kingdom, or California), you are entitled to specific legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data associated with your game account.
  • Right to Rectification: You can modify your player display name directly at any time in the in-game Settings screen.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account, cloud profile, and match history.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your game data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can revoke Google Sign-In access through your Google Account permissions dashboard at any time.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you, deny you game access, or provide lesser gameplay quality for exercising any of your privacy rights.

11 11. User Account & Data Deletion Walkthrough

We respect your right to control your digital footprint. Land Digger.io provides a transparent, immediate self-service deletion option directly inside the game, fully compliant with Google Play’s Account Deletion Policy.

How to Delete Your Account and Data Directly In-Game:

  1. Launch Land Digger.io on your mobile device.
  2. From the Main Lobby, tap the Gear Icon (Settings) located in the upper screen bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Actions section and tap the red "Delete" button (Delete Account & Data).
  4. A confirmation dialog will appear explaining that all cloud records, stats, and coins will be permanently removed. Tap "Delete" to confirm.
  5. Our servers immediately delete your Supabase profile, match logs, and wipe all local device cache.

Alternative Email Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can email our support team at support@devisticvertex.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Land Digger.io" along with your registered Google email address. We will verify your request and permanently delete all records within 7 business days.
